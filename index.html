    usdRig · Unreal Engine 5.8

    The rig you built. Posed in Unreal, exactly.¶

    RigExec runs your baked usdRig character inside Unreal: the same IK, spaces, blend shapes and deformers usdview shows, animated with Control Rig and Sequencer. No re-rigging, no approximations.

    Get started Working in the editor

    Unreal Engine 5.8 on Win64 Control Rig and Sequencer GPU skin, motion vectors, ray tracing USD-space controls

    Built for animators

    Everything the rig does in usdview, inside the editor you animate in.

    • The rig, exactly

      The baked rig evaluates in Unreal as it does in usdview: IK/FK limbs, spaces, blend shapes, wires and every other deformer.

    • USD-space controls

      Every control keeps its USD offset and value. A control's value in Anim Details is the avar usdview shows, so animation always matches the rig.

    • Drawn on the GPU

      Compute-shader normals, motion vectors for motion blur and TSR, and ray-tracing support. If the GPU path isn't available, a CPU fallback takes over and the component tells you so.

    • Animator tools from usdview

      The picker (docked or right in the viewport), Touch Pose, and the right-click marking menu.

    • Sequencer and undo

      Keys go on a Control Rig track like any other rig. Undo brings the rig back exactly, and IK/FK and space switches keep the limb where it is.

    • Works with Squarebit Subdivs

      Add Squarebit Subdivs and the character draws as a subdivision surface on the GPU. Optional; RigExec runs without it.

    The marking menu¶

    Flick toward a command and release: it runs with nothing ever drawn. Hold, and the ring appears so you can read it. Key, reset, zero, frame, switch IK/FK, change space or select the mirrored control, each in a direction your hand learns.

    The marking menu

    Press P over the viewport

    The hover picker¶

    The character's own picker, drawn right over the viewport with no window around it. Move, scale, fade or collapse each tab; everywhere else the viewport still selects, drags gizmos and moves the camera. Your layout comes back next session.

    Touch Pose¶

    Hover the character and the region under the cursor lights; click to select the control it belongs to. Selected regions stay lit. Manipulators always win the click, and Touch Pose steps aside while you drag or play.

    How it works

    The rig is baked once, outside Unreal. Unreal evaluates it; it never rebuilds it.
